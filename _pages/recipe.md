# German Currywurst

## Ingredients

- Sausages (German Wiener, tradiionally)
- Tomato sauce
- Baking soda (very little)
- Curry powder
- Smoked paprika
- Cayenne pepper
- Worcestershire sauce
- Beef broth

## Instructions

- Put the sausages in a pot of cold water and bring it to boil. Remove once cooked.

- [Adjust the portion to fit your taste] Heat up a sauce pan and add tomato sauce and a tiny bit of baking soda (to reduce the acidity). Then season it with curry powder, smoked paprika, cayenne pepper, and onion powder. Add Worcestershire sauce and beef broth to adjust the viscocity.

- Cut up the cooked sausages into bite size and generously coat them with the sauce on top.

## Background

This is a common street food in Berlin. After World War 2, German was in some kind of food scarcity. the American and British soliders that still remain provided two ingredients: tomato sauce (US), and curry powder and Worcestershire sauce (UK, which owned India back then).
