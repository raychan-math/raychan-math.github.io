## DRP Mentor

The Directed Reading Program is a volunteer program in which a graduate student will be paired with one or several undergraduates and guide them through some reading project on certain topic. Starting Fall 2023, the annual program is split into two parts, with a Fall-Winter one-to-one mentoring, and a Spring seminar which can take more students at once.

- Spring 2023: Category Theory (Main reading: Tom Leinster, _Basic Category Theory_)

- Fall 2023 & Winter 2024: Lie groups and Lie algebras (Main reading: Brian Hall, _Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations_)

- Spring 2024: DRP Seminar -- Categories for the beginning mathematicians (Main reading: Tom Leinster, _Basic Category Theory_, Chapter 1, 4, 5 only)

- Fall 2024 & Winter 2025: Moduli space of triangles (Main Reading: Kai Behrend, _Introduction to Algebraic Stacks_, Chapter 1 and 2)

- Spring 2025: DRP Seminar -- The Strongest Shape: Moduli Theory Through the Lens of Triangles
(Main Reading: Kai Behrend, _Introduction to Algebraic Stacks_, Chapter 1 only)

- Fall 2025 & Winter 2026: Moduli spaces and stacks (Main Reading: Barbara Fantechi et al, _Fundamental algebraic geometry: Grothendieck's FGA explained_, Chapter 2 only; supplemented by Behrend's note above)
