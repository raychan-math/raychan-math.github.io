## DRP Mentor

The Directed Reading Program is a volunteer program in which a graduate student will be paired with one or several undergraduates and guide them through some reading project on certain topic. 

- Spring 2023: Mentor

- Fall 2023 & Winter 2024: Lie groups and Lie algebras (Main reading: Brian Hall, _Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations_)

- Spring 2024: DRP Seminar -- Categories for the beginning mathematicians (Main reading: Tom Leinster, _Basic Category Theory_)

- Spring 2025: DRP Seminar -- Moduli spaces of triangles (Main Reading: Kai Behrend, _Introduction to Algebraic Stacks_)

- Fall 2025 & Winter 2026: Moduli spaces and stacks (Main Reading: Barbara Fantechi et al, _Fundamental algebraic geometry: Grothendieck's FGA explained_; supplemented by Behrend's note above)
