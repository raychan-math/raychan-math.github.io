## DRP Mentor

The Directed Reading Program is a volunteer program in which a graduate student will be paired with one or several undergraduates and guide them through some reading project on certain topic. Starting Fall 2023, the annual program is split into two parts, with a Fall-Winter one-to-one mentoring, and a Spring seminar which can take more students at once.

- Spring 2023: Category Theory
    - Main reading: Tom Leinster, _Basic Category Theory_.

- Fall 2023 & Winter 2024: Lie groups and Lie algebras
    - Main reading: Brian Hall, _Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations_.

- Spring 2024: DRP Seminar -- Categories for the beginning mathematicians
    - Main reading: Tom Leinster, _Basic Category Theory_, Chapter 1, 4, 5 only.
    - [Syllabus/proposal](/assets/pdf/Spring_2024_DRP_proposal.pdf).
    - [Exercise](/assets/pdf/Spring_2024_DRP_Exercises.pdf) used for the seminar. (**Note**: This is a huge list, which consists of virtually all exercises on category theory I have in my disposal at that time. I don't ask the participants to do all of them.)

- Fall 2024 & Winter 2025: Moduli space of triangles
    - Main Reading: Kai Behrend, _Introduction to Algebraic Stacks_, Chapter 1 and 2.

- Spring 2025: DRP Seminar -- The Strongest Shape: Moduli Theory Through the Lens of Triangles
    - Main Reading: Kai Behrend, _Introduction to Algebraic Stacks_, Chapter 1 only.
    - This is a rerun of the program from previous two quarters, designed for lower prerequsites and depth.
    - [Syllabus/proposal](/assets/pdf/Spring_2025_DRP_proposal.pdf).
    - [Exercise](/assets/pdf/Spring_2025_DRP_Exercises.pdf) for the seminar.

- Fall 2025 & Winter 2026: Moduli spaces and stacks
    - Main Reading (FGA): Barbara Fantechi et al, _Fundamental algebraic geometry: Grothendieck's FGA explained_, Chapter 2 only
    - We used Behrend's note above before starting FGA.
    - This is essentially a rerun of the Spring 2025 seminar, except we can get more in depth with the longer timeline.
    - [Exercise](/assets/pdf/FGA_Explained_Exercise.pdf) for the FGA reading. Please note that in order to use it properly, someone must redacted the book appropriately first (as some exercise asks you to reproduce a proof, or work on an example).
