Want to have something to lighten up your day? Or you want to impress someone in a party? You are in the right place then. As you might know by now, this page enlists facts that you might never know of. Some of them are humorous, some are pointless.

The date in each entry just indicates the date I learned this fact. It is purely there for organizational prupose. "XX" means I don't remember the exact date I obtain it.

- XX/XX/2025: The US steals many city names from Canada. The two most prominant examples are Vancouver, Washington (which I discovered during a stopover at Portland, after a conference down in Eugene, Oregon) as well as Ontario, California (which I discovered when my UPS shipment routed through there).

- 02/XX/2026: The Earth is the densest planet within the solar system.

- 02/XX/2026: The Cincinnati International Airport is geographically located in Kentucky, making it an airport that doesn't locate within the state containing the city that it gets its name from. (Cincinnati is in Ohio, not Kentucky.) Nowadays they amend the name to include Northern Kentucky as well.
On that spirit, the Dulles International airport is located in Virginia but is advertised as serving mainly people in Washington DC. This is even more mysterious than Cincinnatti since Washington DC is not a state (as of 2026).

- 03/06/2026: A telegram is an old technology used to transmit word messages in far distance, while a telegraph is the machine that transmits telegrams. (I bet you don't know there is a distinction, right?)

- 03/06/2026: A numismatist is a habbitist who collect coins. (In other words, numismatist = coin collector.)

- 03/10/2026: Phobophobia is the fear of having a fear. (Therefore, recursively, we can define what phobophobophobophobophobophobophobophobophobophobophobophobophobophobophobophbia is; don't read this if you have a fear of long words, aka hippopotomonstrosesquipedaliophobia.)

- 03/11/2026: The color turquoise ("tur-quoi") is different than the color cyan.

- 03/13/2026: Arachibutyrophobia is the fear of peanut butter sticking on the roof of the mouth.

- 03/13/2026: A cyanometer is used to measure how blue the sky is (although cyan is not really blue).

- 03/13/2026: Wisconsin boarders both the Lake Superior and the Lake Michigan, both of which are among the largest lakes in the world.

- 04/10/2026: A sphygmomanometer ("sfig-mo-ma-no-me-ter") is the machine that you use every time going to your doctor's office; it is that machine that measures your blood pressure!

- 04/13/2026: Have you remember the last time you were having this big board full of salami, ham, cheese, grape, biscuit, etc.? (We have them every time in our Department Colloquium.) Do you know what it is called? A charcuterie ("sha-ku-te-ri").
